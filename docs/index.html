How reliable it has been: the registered backtest, November vintage

The November vintage decides pass or fail. A hit means the index called the sign of the actual yield against the stage-1 expectation. MAE is mean absolute error in percentage points, against the zero-anomaly baseline. Each version has its own registered marks, and there is no combined verdict.

Limits

  • The index tracks growing conditions, not yield.
  • The private trade houses have field crop tours and farm data that this project does not.
  • MODIS at 250 m blurs Zona da Mata's steep smallholder terrain. The coffee mask under-represents its smallholder and shaded coffee.
  • Frost comes from INMET screen-height stations, which sample cold-air pools poorly. They did not flag the July 2021 frost in Sul de Minas or São Paulo.
